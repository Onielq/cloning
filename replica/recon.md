## 3. Flows

1. **Scroll narrative:** visitor scrolls → headline reveals → story pins and advances steps 01→04 → progress dots track → closing lands the pitch.
2. **Dot navigation:** clicking a progress dot smooth-scrolls to that step.
3. **Nav wayfinding:** navbar recolors by zone; dropdown affordances (▾) for Product/Markets/Insights/Resources.
4. **Conversion:** any path ends at the Contact CTA (nav) / footer contact.
5. **Media view:** open/play a walkthrough clip from the showcase strip.

## 4. Components

Navbar (pill, blur, themed states) · Logo lockup · Dropdown link · Ghost/solid CTA ·
Grid background layer · Scallop divider (SVG) · Animated headline (word mask reveal) ·
Sticky story panel (step num, rotating titles) · SVG scene viz (truck, lanes, reticle,
scan line, badges) · Progress dots · Closing title (line-mask reveal) · Video card ·
Footer columns.

## 6. Feature matrix

See `replica/features.csv`. Must-haves: scroll story with 4 pinned steps, themed
navbar, scallop dividers, word-reveal typography, reduced-motion fallback,
responsive down to 375 px. Nice-to-have: custom cursor, parallax grid, clickable
progress dots. Out of scope: any backend/CRM form handling, real product screens.
